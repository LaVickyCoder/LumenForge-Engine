# Commercial Direction

LumenForge is being developed with both **internal** and **external** use in mind.

## Internal use

The engine is intended to support games developed within the LumenForge ecosystem. Internal development may use proprietary systems, tooling, integrations, and infrastructure that are not part of the public repository.

## External use

A future external offering is planned. Possible models include commercial licensing, subscriptions, or differentiated editions for individual developers, indie teams, studios, and larger organizations.

The final licensing model, feature tiers, pricing, source-access policy, support terms, and redistribution rights have **not** been finalized.

## What this public repository means

Public visibility does not grant an open-source license to proprietary LumenForge technology.

Unless a file or component explicitly states otherwise, no permission is granted to copy, modify, redistribute, sublicense, or commercially use LumenForge materials beyond rights required for ordinary viewing through GitHub.

See [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md).

## Future announcements

When an external SDK, runtime, preview, community edition, or commercial product becomes available, its exact license and usage terms will be published with that release.
